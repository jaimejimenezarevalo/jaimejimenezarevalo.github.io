I am a first-generation undergraduate mathematics student at California State University, Dominguez Hills, where I study mathematics and conduct research in coding theory and combinatorics. My current work includes decoding polar affine Grassmann codes and investigating the relationship between Liar's Bingo and error-correcting codes.

I am especially interested in using proof, computation, and mathematical experimentation to understand abstract structures. Alongside my research, I work as a tutor and supplemental instructor, helping students build confidence in subjects ranging from introductory mathematics to differential equations and linear algebra. My long-term goal is to obtain a Ph.D. in mathematics, contribute to mathematical research, and expand access to academic opportunities for students from underrepresented communities.
